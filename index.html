How We Work

Assessment & Analysis

Understand Processes

  • We learn your business's goal first to know high impact operations that determine what your business success is.
  • We outline processes that should be automated and operations that stay human.
  • We assess processes that should be automated and merge tasks that are similar.
  • We map the required tech stack and automation building process.

Build the Architecture

Connect Apps & APIs

  • We utilize AI expertise to decide where AI agents will be most effective and integrate tightly.
  • We build custom workflows with conditional logic and branching, tested across different scenarios and edge cases.
  • We build a guardrail - an error workflow that alerts instantly if an app or API doesn't respond in production, instead of silence.
  • We build live websites and web apps when requested.

Deployment & Shipping

Retainer or one-off build

  • We deploy the system into production with measured data of success.
  • We continuously manage and collect feedback on usage to iterate.
  • We continuously audit and refine tech stack, logic, and models to stay relevant as your business evolves.
  • We hand over export files and documentation for one-off builds.
